# Problem: Two Sum
Difficulty: Easy

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

Use two loops to check every pair of elements in the array.
If the sum of two elements equals the target, return their indices.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

## Notes

The answer requires the indices of the two numbers, not the numbers themselves.
